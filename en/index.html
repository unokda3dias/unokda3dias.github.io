01 / The project

An edition conceived for each artist.

Art does not end with the artwork. The gaze, the words and the time we devote to looking matter too.

UnoKda_3_Días is an independent initiative for digital art outreach and mediation. It researches artistic careers, studies works and develops curatorial publications with a distinct editorial language.

The artwork retains its authorship and integrity. Design, research and curatorial texts form a separate editorial practice. We do not act as a commercial representing gallery.

Curatorial and editorial direction: Jorge Boullosa Trejo · BULHOSART

05 / Curatorial notebook

Art does not end with the artwork.

A figure, the tension of a line or the trace of a hand on a material can make us pause. We do not always know why. Looking again brings us closer to the creative process.

This space will bring together observations, questions and curatorial readings that invite us to look without imposing a single interpretation.

Jorge Boullosa Trejo · BULHOSART
