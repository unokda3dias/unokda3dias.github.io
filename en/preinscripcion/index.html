The process

Four steps, no fees or advance commitments.

  1. 01

    Read the conditions

    Learn about the project and what submitting an application entails. It does not mean acceptance or a confirmed date.

  2. 02

    Prepare your files

    Gather images of your works and artistic documents in your own Google Drive folder.

  3. 03

    Submit an application

    When submissions open, you will complete a free form linked from this website.

  4. 04

    Await review

    The project director will review the application and may request clarification. Selection and any later publication require separate decisions and permissions.

Evaluation materials

Your artistic documentation.

Organize the materials in your own Google Drive folder. Do not upload private files to GitHub or to any public area of the website.

01

Artworks

Twenty numbered JPG/JPEG photographs preserving the appearance of each work. Approximately 2,000 pixels on the longest side are recommended.

02

Portrait

One artistic portrait for reviewing the application, without automatic permission to publish it.

03

CV

A summary of your professional and artistic career, without identity documents or unnecessary official details.

04

Biography

A biographical introduction to be used as background material.

05

Artwork details

Title, year, technique, dimensions and relevant notes. An additional audiovisual link may be provided where appropriate.

About Google Drive: the folder remains yours. When applications open, you will grant private read-only access to the official account specified. We do not recommend sharing your documents through a public link.

Application request

Apply without a submission fee.

The planned form will ask for your name, artistic name, email, city and country, discipline, an introduction to your work, your motivation and a link to your artistic documentation.

It will include an authorship declaration and information on data processing. No payment or bank details will be requested.

Frequently asked questions

Before you apply.

Is pre-registration free?

Yes. Submitting an application is free. This website does not process payments or billing.

Does applying reserve a place in the next edition?

No. Joining the applicant pool does not guarantee a place, a scheduled date or publication.

May I submit work in different disciplines?

Yes. You may describe your artistic practice and add relevant supporting links.

Will my images and documents be published automatically?

No. Submission allows private review only. Any publication requires separate authorization.

Where will my files be stored?

Initially, your files remain in your own Google Drive folder, with private read-only access. The project’s internal archive will be configured and tested before applications open.

Has a date for the next edition been announced?

No. Future editions will be announced once the director confirms their schedules and conditions.
